## 🏆 The Four Championship Seasons

</div>

<br/>

### 🏆 2021 — The Maiden Title

<div align="center">

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/2021_Red_Bull_RB16B.jpg" width="800" alt="Red Bull RB16B 2021 — Max Verstappen First Championship Car"/>

<sub><b>🔴 Red Bull RB16B (2021)</b> · Honda RA621H · Driver #33 → #1 · <b>10 race wins</b></sub>

</div>

> **🏁 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix — Season Finale:** In one of the most dramatic moments in F1 history, Verstappen overtook Lewis Hamilton on the **very last lap of the last race** to win his first World Drivers' Championship. He became the **first Dutch F1 World Champion** ever. The season came down to the final lap of the final race, with both drivers level on points going into Abu Dhabi.
>
> **📋 Season Stats:** 10 Wins · 18 Podiums · 10 Poles · 395.5 Points

---

### 🏆 2022 — Ground Effect Dominance

<div align="center">

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/RB18_JapaneseGP_FP2_(cropped).jpg" width="800" alt="Red Bull RB18 2022 — Max Verstappen Second Championship Car"/>

<sub><b>🔴 Red Bull RB18 (2022)</b> · Honda RBPTH001 · Driver #1 · <b>15 race wins</b></sub>

</div>

> **🏁 Japanese Grand Prix:** Verstappen clinched his second title at Suzuka in the rain with **15 victories** — smashing his own record for most wins in a season (set the following year). Red Bull also won the **Constructors' Championship** for the first time since 2013. Verstappen scored **454 championship points** — the highest single-season total in F1 history at the time.
>
> **📋 Season Stats:** 15 Wins · 17 Podiums · 17 Poles · 454 Points

---

### 🏆 2023 — The Greatest Season in F1 History

<div align="center">

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Max_Verstappen_Red_Bull_RB19_Petersen_Automotive_Museum.jpg" width="800" alt="Red Bull RB19 2023 — Most Dominant F1 Car Ever Built"/>

<sub><b>🔴 Red Bull RB19 (2023)</b> · Honda RBPTH002 · Driver #1 · <b>19 race wins (all-time record)</b> · <b>95.45% win rate</b></sub>

</div>

> **🏁 The Historic Season:** The RB19 annihilated the competition. 19 wins from 22 races shattered McLaren MP4/4's 1988 record as the highest win-percentage car in F1 history. Verstappen also set the record for **10 consecutive race victories** and became the only driver in history to win **21 podiums in a single season**. The dominance was so total, pundits debated whether it was bad for the sport.
>
> **📋 Season Stats:** 19 Wins · 21 Podiums · 12 Poles · 575 Points

---

### 🏆 2024 — The Gritty Fourth Crown

<div align="center">

<img src="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/FIA_F1_Austria_2023_Race_(2).jpg" width="800" alt="Max Verstappen Red Bull Racing Action Shot"/>

<sub><b>🔴 Red Bull RB20 (2024)</b> · Honda RBPTH002 · Driver #1 · <b>9 race wins</b></sub>

</div>

> **🏁 Brazilian Grand Prix — The Masterclass:** With Red Bull no longer the fastest car, Verstappen delivered arguably his **greatest-ever race** in São Paulo. Starting **17th in torrential rain**, he drove to victory in a breathtaking display, passing the entire field. He clinched the fourth title at the Las Vegas Grand Prix by out-scoring Lando Norris. He became the **first driver to win a championship with a 3rd-placed constructor** since Nelson Piquet in 1983.
>
> **📋 Season Stats:** 9 Wins · 14 Podiums · 12 Poles · 437 Points

---

### 2025 — Lost by Two Points 💔

> **🏁 The Heartbreaker:** Verstappen came within **2 points** of a 5th consecutive title. After being **104 points behind** Norris at the Dutch GP, he staged the greatest championship comeback in F1 history — winning 8 races in the second half of the season. He won the Abu Dhabi finale from pole, but Norris's P3 was just enough. The most dramatic title fight in years.
>
> **📋 Season Stats:** 8 Wins · 15 Podiums · P2 WDC
